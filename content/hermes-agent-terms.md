# Hermes Agent Terms of Use

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Hermes Agent is a privately operated assistant for accounts authorized by Gérard Paligot. It is not offered for general public registration or use. Access to Google services requires the account owner's consent through Google's authorization flow.

The assistant acts on requests made through its configured interfaces. Its output can be incomplete or inaccurate, and actions such as sending mail or editing files can affect the authorized account. The account owner remains responsible for reviewing instructions and results, and may revoke Google access at any time in their [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

Service availability depends on the VPS, Google APIs, and the configured AI services. For questions, contact [gerard@paligot.com](mailto:gerard@paligot.com). Data handling is described in the [privacy policy](/hermes-agent/privacy/).
