Hermes Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Hermes Agent is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant operated by Gérard Paligot. This policy explains how it handles Google user data for accounts whose owner has granted access.

Data accessed

Depending on the permissions granted and the task requested, Hermes Agent may access Gmail messages and metadata; Google Drive files and metadata; Google Calendar events; Google Contacts; Google Docs; and Google Sheets. It also receives OAuth credentials needed to maintain authorized access. A requested permission does not mean every service is available or used for every task.

How data is used

Hermes Agent uses Google data to answer the account owner’s requests and carry out requested actions, such as finding or summarizing mail, drafting or sending a message, and working with files or calendar information. It does not use Google user data to serve advertisements or sell that data.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the privately operated VPS so authorized access can continue between sessions. Task results and relevant Google data may also appear in the assistant’s conversation history, logs, or backups. There is no automatic fixed deletion period for these records. The account owner can revoke the application’s access in their Google Account and contact the operator to request deletion of stored credentials or related records. Revocation prevents new access but does not itself erase information already processed or backed up.

Sharing and external processing

Hermes Agent sends requests to Google APIs to perform authorized tasks. Relevant content may be sent to the AI model services configured for the assistant when a task requires interpretation or generation. The VPS hosting provider and those AI service providers may process data as part of operating the service. Hermes Agent is not offered as a public data-sharing service, and Google user data is not sold.

Your choices and contact

You can review or revoke access in your Google Account permissions. For questions or deletion requests, contact gerard@paligot.com.
